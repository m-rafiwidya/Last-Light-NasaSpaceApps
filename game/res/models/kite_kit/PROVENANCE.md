# Kite kit provenance

Kite is built by `tools/blender/scripts/kite/make_kite.py` from this project's own rover kit meshes
(`tools/blender/parts/*.blend`, generated by `tools/blender/scripts/make_*.py`) in a damaged pose
solved on the Apollo 12 terrain (`game/res/terrain/moon_apollo12/play.f32`). Its service port bracket
is an original design made by the same script with the workshop kit's materials. No borrowed or
AI-generated meshes, no textures, no third-party assets. Source: `tools/blender/parts/kite/kite.blend`.

The diagnostic cartridge and the cargo mount are rover kit parts (`game/res/models/rover_kit/`),
generated by `make_cartridge.py` and `make_cargo_mount.py` in `tools/blender/scripts/kite/`.

Scale and collision were reviewed by the generators' checks (sizes, clearances on the complete
rover, Kite's contact with the terrain, the arm's delivery path); see their `KITE_KIT ...` lines.
