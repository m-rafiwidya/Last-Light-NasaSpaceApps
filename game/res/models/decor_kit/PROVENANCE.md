# Decor kit provenance

Every piece here is an original design, generated by script in Blender 5.2 from the numbers in
`tools/blender/DECOR_KIT.md`. No borrowed or AI-generated meshes and no textures in the models.
The only third-party content of the decor is the two NASA photographs on the history cards,
kept apart in `game/res/textures/decor/` (see its `PROVENANCE.md`); Godot puts them on the cards'
`card_photo` quads. No words are modelled: Godot draws them from `game/res/decor/decor_texts.json`.
Sources: `tools/blender/parts/decor/<asset>.blend`.

| Piece | Generator |
|---|---|
| `dc_tool_chest`, `dc_crate`, `dc_cable_reel`, `dc_spare_panels`, `dc_wheel_spare`, `dc_ladder`, `dc_o2_rack`, `dc_extinguisher`, `dc_threshold_band`, `dc_sign_warning`, `dc_sign_oxidiser`, `dc_sign_voltage` | `tools/blender/scripts/decor/make_decor_bay.py` |
| `dc_mug`, `dc_tablet`, `dc_plant_lab`, `dc_service_note`, `dc_history_card` | `make_decor_crew.py` |
| `dc_junction_box` | `make_decor_exterior.py` |
| `dc_layout` (empties only), `dc_interior` (the interior pieces joined) | `make_decor_layout.py` |

Scale, clearances and collision were reviewed by the generators' own checks: dimensions,
triangle budgets, the workshop's assembled checks (the rover's lanes, U-turn and dock, the
player's walkways and reach) with every decor piece in place, the interact triggers' 5 cm, the
sight lines to every reach target, and the exterior lane; see the scripts' `DECOR_KIT ...` report
lines.
